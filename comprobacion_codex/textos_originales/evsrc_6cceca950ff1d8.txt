BEST DEEP DISH PIZZA IN CHICAGO Burt Katz opened the original Pequod’s Pizza in Morton Grove, Illinois, in 1971. A local legend in the realm of Chicago pizza, Katz was responsible for starting a cult following for our signature caramelized crust that Pequod’s is still known for today. Our pizzeria’s famous pan pie is regularly lauded as the best deep dish pizza in Chicago. Place your order now if you’re ready to find out why. Read More About Us Our Famous Caramelized Crust Pequod’s has a stellar reputation for offering some of the best pizza in Chicago, which is no small feat in a city brimming with saucy, cheesy, piled-high pies. So how exactly does Pequod’s Pizza achieve this feat? With a creative crust so mouthwatering you have to taste it to believe it. If you’re a cheese-lover, you’ll almost certainly agree that Pequod’s has the best Chicago pizza, as this savory delight comes baked right into the famous crust. The pizza artists at Pequod’s begin by placing a ring of mozzarella around the rim of the deep dish pan which caramelizes as the pie bakes. This is followed up with a hearty helping of freshly made dough that plumps up into a mouthwatering crust that’s focaccia-like in its airy consistency. The pizza comes out with a dark rim that’s a seemingly impossible combination of crispy and chewy all in one decadent bite. It’s this rich, enticing crust that’s the staple feature of Pequod’s Pizza. Whether you pile on the pepperoni or go wild for veggies , this deep caramelized crust is at the heart of every piece of Pequod’s deep dish pizza. Morton Grove Chicago Famous, Scrumptious Pizza in Morton Grove Pequod’s Pizza is one of the most famous recipients of Burt Katz’s culinary genius, but it’s far from the only Chicago destination to bear his fingerprints. Katz began with Gulliver’s Pizza & Pub, which operated in Rogers Park. He worked there for six years, then sold his stake in Gulliver’s so he could move on to a fresh new challenge, which became Pequod’s. The original Morton Grove destination is easily accessible for travelers passing through Chicago. This location also offers convenient delivery options for Morton Grove residents. If you prefer pickup, just pull into the lot and a pizza valet will bring your pie to the car to make carryout a cinch.
