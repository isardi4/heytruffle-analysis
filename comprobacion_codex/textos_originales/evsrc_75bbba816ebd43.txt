A Little Bit About Us We’re known for our award-winning pizza, with over 250 ‘Best Pizza’ accolades! Zachary’s opened our original location on College Avenue in 1983. Today, we proudly serve the East Bay with six locations: Oakland-Rockridge, Oakland-Grand Lake, North Berkeley, Pleasant Hill, Pleasanton, and Livermore. Our team is made up of a diverse and talented group of dedicated employees and employee-owners. We take pride in our exceptional employee retention, with around 25 team members having over 20 years of Zachary’s experience, and 50 with over 10 years.
