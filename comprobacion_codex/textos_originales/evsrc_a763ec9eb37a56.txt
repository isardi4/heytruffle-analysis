Alongside family members, the couple opened the first location on Mack Road in Sacramento, CA. Thanks to their dedication and love for authentic Mexican cuisine, Super Taco has since expanded to various locations throughout Northern California. Their culinary passion has been passed down through generations, and today, Super Taco remains a proudly family-owned and operated business.
