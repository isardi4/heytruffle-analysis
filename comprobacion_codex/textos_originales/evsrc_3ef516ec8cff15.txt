Got a question? CHECK OUT OUR FAQs! Can I Make A Reservation? We do not take reservations, as we operate on a first come, first serve basis. We serve food until scheduled closing time unless we sell out early. Allergens Nuts: No nuts of any kind are ever used in our kitchen. However, our sauces and rubs are manufactured in facilities that may handle products with nuts. Gluten: We are very gluten friendly and can accommodate most allergen issues. All of our meats are gluten free except our Burnt Ends. Some of our sides are gluten free, please call the store to find out which ones. Our Sweet Baby Jane Sauce and Carolina Vinegar are gluten free. Have additional allergen questions? Give us a call at 314.535.4340. Do You Ship Sauces, Rubs and Swag? Yes! You can order our famous rubs, sauces, t-shirts and hats for nationwide deliver. Shop now Can I Bring a Big Group To Pappy's? We always want to know when big groups are coming in so we can plan accordingly. We can accommodate almost any size group; and we ask that the entire party is present before joining the line as this is the only way to seat your group together. TIP: If your group is between 20-64, you may want to reserve our private banquet room. Learn more Do You Deliver? We do not deliver; however, you can order Pappy's for delivery through Uber Eats.
