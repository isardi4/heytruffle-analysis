Danielle - 2023 ” I started working for SPIN! Pizza shortly after I turned 18 years old as a server/cashier. I was then trained to be an hourly shift leader and really enjoyed the additional responsibilities. About 3 years later, a payroll position opened up in the corporate office, and I was extremely excited to be given the opportunity to expand my knowledge and take a different path within the company. The payroll position quickly broadened to other learning opportunities such as health insurance and 401k benefits, unemployment claims, employment verifications, HR scenarios, etc. As the company grew, so did the ability to learn new things and take on more responsibilities. The best thing about my job is the people I get to work with on a daily basis. My co-workers are an extension to my family and we work well together as a team. It's really cool to still be working with several people that I worked with in the very beginning of my SPIN! Pizza career. I had always heard growing up that if you find a job you love, you'll never have to work a day in your life and that couldn't be any more true. Currently going on 13 years working for SPIN! Pizza and there is nowhere else I'd rather be. Danielle Payroll/HR/Benefits START YOUR SPIN! JOURNEY START YOUR SPIN! JOURNEY Privacy Policy Terms of Use Sitemap Catering Spin!
